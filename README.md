01 / A little about me
I’m a BS Information Technology student at PHINMA University of Pangasinan with a focus on front-end web development, mobile development, and UI/UX design. I like taking a project from the first wireframe to a working interface, paying attention to both the details users see and the logic that makes a product useful.
- Design-minded: I care about visual hierarchy, consistency, accessibility, and clear user flows.
- Build-oriented: I enjoy turning concepts and prototypes into functional web and mobile experiences.
- Always learning: I’m continuously improving my development workflow, problem-solving, and product thinking.
My approach: Make it clear. Make it useful. Make it feel good to use.
